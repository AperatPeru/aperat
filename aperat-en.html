About APERAT

Peruvian Association of Atheists

Who We Are

Founded on November 4, 2009, in Lima, the Peruvian Association of Atheists (APERAT) is a non-profit civil organization. APERAT represents and provides spaces for action, dialogue, and community building for atheists, agnostics, secular humanists, and freethinkers in Peru.

Our Core Pillars

Scientism
We adopt a scientific approach as the most reliable way to acquire knowledge, applying reason and empirical inquiry to understanding the human experience and the natural world.
Secular Humanism
As human beings are responsible for giving meaning to their own lives, we promote ethical values based on reason, empathy, and human rights, without relying on supernatural beliefs.
Laicism / Secular State
We advocate for a strictly secular state that guarantees absolute freedom of conscience, equality before the law, and non-discrimination for all citizens, regardless of their religious beliefs.
Progressivism
We support social liberty, sexual and reproductive rights, and equal civil rights for all communities, rejecting conservatism rooted in dogmatic religious traditions.

Global Connections

APERAT is actively engaged in international advocacy. We are proud members of Humanists International (formerly IHEU) and Atheist Alliance International, working with global partners to promote secular values and protect freethinkers.
