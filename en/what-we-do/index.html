What We Do

Civic action, legal advocacy, and critical thinking

We actively work across four core pillars to build a freer, fairer, and more rational society in Peru.

Our 4 pillars of institutional action

We structure our initiatives to defend civil rights, educate in rational thinking, and actively advocate before state institutions and society.

01 / DEFEND

Defending freedom of conscience

We provide free guidance, support, and legal counsel against religious discrimination, confessional impositions in schools or universities, and violations of the rights of non-believers.

02 / PROMOTE

Promoting scientific culture

We foster the scientific method, critical thinking, and secular education. We counter misinformation, pseudoscience, and obscurantist dogmas through educational events, conferences, and public outreach.

03 / ADVANCE

Advancing state secularism

We monitor the institutional neutrality of the state apparatus and promote legislative and civic initiatives to dismantle unwarranted religious privileges, repeal obsolete concordats, and ensure a neutral, independent state.

04 / BUILD

Building community & ceremonies

We create safe, welcoming spaces for fellowship, dialogue, and mutual support among non-believers and humanists. We conduct secular ceremonies (legally recognized weddings and memorials) with dignity, warmth, and free from dogma.

Historic International Event

Global humanism comes to Lima

From August 20 to 22, 2027, Lima will host the World Humanist Congress, the triennial gathering bringing together humanist organizations, scholars, and grassroots activists from across all five continents.

The Congress is organized by Humanists International in partnership with the Peruvian Association of Atheists. A historic milestone positioning Peru as a global crossroads for reason, human rights, and freedom of conscience.
