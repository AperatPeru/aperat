About Us

A Peruvian organization
with an international history

The Peruvian Association of Atheists (APERAT) is a Peruvian humanist organization founded in 2009.

We work to promote critical thinking, defend freedom of conscience, protect the rights of non-religious people, and advocate for a secular state.

Our history is linked to the development of the atheist, humanist, and freethought movement in Peru and Latin America.

What do we understand by humanism?

Secular humanism starts from a simple conviction: human beings can build ethical, meaningful, and compassionate lives through our own capacities, without relying on supernatural mandates.

Reason, experience, empathy, and knowledge allow us to understand the world, make decisions, and take responsibility for their consequences. Humanism promotes ethical living, personal fulfilment, and a source of meaning compatible with freedom of thought and human diversity.

Every person’s dignity is independent of their beliefs. Humanism therefore also entails responsibility towards others, future generations, and the natural environment we share.

Timeline since 2009

Milestones in our work to build a humanist, secular, and freethinking community with an international outlook.

2009

Founding of the Peruvian Association of Atheists.

2010–2017

Development of outreach activities, meetings, and initiatives related to atheism, humanism, and secularism.

2018

Arequipa hosts the first Latin American Freethought Gathering, with participation of regional humanist and freethought organizations.

2018–2026

Participation of APERAT in successive Latin American Freethought Gatherings and in international humanist movement activities.

2023

First Atheist and Humanist Gathering in Lima, supported by Humanists International.

2026

APERAT continues developing activities in Peru and actively participating in the international humanist movement.

2027

Lima will host the World Humanist Congress.

APERAT

Part of a
global movement

APERAT is a member of Humanists International, the global organization bringing together humanist organizations from around the world to promote humanism, defend human rights, and represent humanist voices internationally.

We actively participate in international forums, including its General Assemblies and conferences.

Our Vice President, Adrián Núñez, serves on the Board of Directors of Humanists International.

In 2027, this connection will hold special significance: Lima will host the World Humanist Congress, with APERAT collaborating in its local organization.
