More than 17 years building community

Founded in Lima in 2009, APERAT is the first legally established organization for atheists, agnostics, and humanists in Peru. We promote critical thinking, the unwavering defense of a Secular State, and universal human rights in collaboration with national and international networks like Humanists International.

Between 2010 and 2020 we hosted the radio show Para-Normales de la Noche on FM Radio Canto Grande, applying skepticism to pseudotherapies, astrology, and charlatanism. Since 2025 we continue this work with La Señal Humanista, focusing on human rights and democracy. We also produced the documentary Is Peru a Secular State? (2024–2025) with the support of Humanists International.

We provide free legal counsel to individuals facing discrimination for their convictions. Our Exonérate Perú campaign helps families exercise their statutory right to exempt children from confessional religious instruction in school. Since 2010 we have guided those who wish to formally apostatize from the Catholic Church, helping hundreds regain control over their personal records.

In 2024 we established Ceremonias Laicas, featuring celebrants trained under international standards. We conducted the first recorded humanist ceremony in Peru (2023) and offer humanist weddings with civil legal validity through partner notaries. These ceremonies center on people, reason, and shared empathy.

Since 2026 we stage nearly weekly street theater performances on Sundays across various community settlements in Lima. The plays use satire to explore the relationship between politics, religion, and democracy, accompanied by community donations for local residents.

Our principles

Ethical, philosophical, and civic pillars guiding our activism for a more just, rational, and free society.

01 / SCIENCE

Reason and critical thinking

We seek to understand the world through evidence, reason, and testable scientific knowledge.

02 / CONSCIENCE

Freedom of conscience

Every person must be able to form, change, or maintain their own convictions without coercion or discrimination.

03 / SECULAR STATE

Secular state and equality

Public institutions must act with independence and neutrality. No religion should enjoy state privileges.

04 / RIGHTS

Universal human rights

We defend a society founded on the inalienable dignity, equality, and freedom of all individuals.

Historic International Gathering

Global humanism arrives in Lima

From August 20 to 22, 2027, Lima will host the World Humanist Congress, the premier global gathering bringing together humanist organizations, scholars, and activists from all five continents.

The Congress is organized by Humanists International in collaboration with the Peruvian Association of Atheists. A historic milestone positioning Peru as a crossroad for reason, human rights, and freedom of conscience.
